# Chapter 8: The Queen’s Word

**Allahabad, India**
**November 1, 1858**


Arjun looked at the horizon. The sun was setting, casting long, dark shadows over the plains. 

"Is this the end, kaka?"

Bishan looked at Arjun, then at the young men who were listening to them from the shadows. "It is the end of the Great Awakening, son. But it is the beginning of something deeper. We have shown that the Company can be broken. We have shown that India has a voice. The fire is not dead; it has just gone underground. It will wait. And one day, it will rise again, not as a mutiny of a few, but as the movement of a nation."

Arjun Singh took off his red Company jacket and folded it for the last time. He walked toward his village in Awadh, no longer a sepoy of a foreign power, but a man who had seen the first light of a long-delayed dawn.

### 🛡️ The History Check (Fact File)

*   **The Government of India Act (1858):** The rule of the East India Company was abolished. Power was transferred to the **British Crown** (Queen Victoria).
*   **The Proclamation (Nov 1, 1858):** Read by **Lord Canning** at Allahabad.
    *   The **Doctrine of Lapse** was ended.
    *   No more territorial annexations.
    *   Religious freedom and equality for all Indians were promised.
*   **The Viceroy:** The Governor-General was now called the Viceroy, representing the Queen.
*   **Secretary of State:** A new office in London to oversee Indian affairs.
*   **Army Reform:** The ratio of European to Indian soldiers was increased. Artillery was kept strictly in European hands.
*   **Divide and Rule:** The British started a policy of creating distrust between Hindus and Muslims to prevent another united uprising.
